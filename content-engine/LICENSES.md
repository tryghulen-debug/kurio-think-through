# Attribution and licenses

The 12 included introductory stories are newly authored demonstration text. Their 12 illustrated visuals were generated for this app; they are not copied from the Deepstash advertisement or other applications.

Automated articles use **Danish Wikipedia** text under **CC BY-SA 4.0** and link to a revision's source URL in the reading view. Wikipedia attribution/CC BY-SA terms must be preserved if the feed is shared, and applicable adaptation terms may apply. Please verify every article for accuracy before public launch. The update script does not paraphrase with AI; it divides source excerpts into mobile slides.

Automated pictures are downloaded from **Wikimedia Commons** only if the API declares CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0, or Public Domain. Every item keeps image creator, license string and Commons source URL, shown under its article. Automated images are stored as resized WebP to avoid hotlinking and reduce data transfer; CC BY-SA adaptation/attribution obligations may apply. Some image licenses require more specificity; review the images and their original files before commercial publication.

If license metadata is missing or the download fails, the app uses a category-colored **abstract SVG generated locally**. Abstract SVGs are concept art, not technical diagrams; they must not be presented as scientifically accurate illustrations.

The updater is a prototype. User-agent/contact information, accuracy, license compliance, API traffic and Google Play policy must be reviewed by the publisher.
